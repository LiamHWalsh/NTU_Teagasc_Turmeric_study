ANALYSIS METHODS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Two complementary approaches were used:

1. LINEAR MIXED MODELS (LMM) or LINEAR MODELS (LM)
   • Accounts for repeated measures (participant as random effect)
   • Tests main effects and interactions
   • Model selection based on data structure:
     - LMM if sufficient repeated measures
     - LM if insufficient data for random effects

2. TIMEPOINT-SPECIFIC TESTS
   • Independent analysis at each timepoint
   • Parametric (t-test) or non-parametric (Wilcoxon) based on normality
   • Effect sizes (Cohen's d) calculated
   • FDR correction for multiple comparisons

EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN:
  • Treatment groups: Control vs. Turmeric
  • Timepoints: Baseline, 2 weeks, 6 months
  • Metrics: Richness (q=0), Shannon (q=1), Simpson (q=2)
  • Excluded: Participants who stopped turmeric treatment

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
INTERPRETATION GUIDE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

LMM/LM Results:
  • Fixed effects show overall impact of treatment and time
  • Interaction terms (if present) show whether treatment effects vary by timepoint
  • p < 0.05 indicates statistical significance

Timepoint-Specific Results:
  • Direct comparisons between groups at each timepoint
  • q-values (FDR-adjusted) control for multiple testing
  • Cohen's d effect sizes: 0.2 (small), 0.5 (medium), 0.8 (large)
